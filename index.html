How it works

Dawaya operates a high-resilience 5-stage pharmacy workflow connecting AI vision, catalog fuzzy search, and automated inventory management:

1
Omnichannel Customer Intake (WhatsApp & Telegram)

Receives text messages or prescription photos from customers. The message is normalized and classified to handle images and raw text requests simultaneously.

📱 WhatsApp Trigger + ✈️ Telegram Trigger → Normalization Message → Is Photo?
2
Multimodal Prescription Vision OCR (Google Gemini)

If the patient uploads a handwritten prescription image, the workflow downloads the media from WhatsApp/Telegram servers and prompts Google Gemini to extract medicine names, strengths, and dosages.

📥 Download Media → Prepare Prescription Read → 👁️ AI Read Request (Gemini) → 📊 Read Catalog (Sheets)
3
Smart Catalog Matching & Levenshtein Fuzzy Search

Matches extracted medications against the Google Sheets catalog using Levenshtein distance on brand names and active ingredients. If an item is out of stock, it automatically discovers the cheapest in-stock alternative with identical active ingredient and strength.

Match Catalog (JS Engine) → Controlled Drug Gate → Cheapest Alt Recommender → Switch Router
4
Automated Reservation vs. Pharmacist Review

Unambiguous in-stock items are auto-reserved: stock is decremented and a 2-hour hold confirmation with total price in EGP is dispatched to the patient. Controlled narcotics or ambiguous items are sent to the Pharmacist Telegram channel with inline approve/reject buttons.

Route: Auto Reserve → 📊 Append Reservation → 📦 Decrement Stock → ✉️ WhatsApp Confirmation
5
2-Hour Expiry Watchdog & Restock Automation (Dawaya Error)

A scheduled watchdog checks active reservations every 15 minutes. Unclaimed medications after 2 hours (Cairo time) expire automatically, returning stock to the shelf and notifying the patient.

⏰ 15-Min Expiration Watchdog → Expired Lines Filter → Restock Totals → 📦 Return Stock → ⚠️ Low Stock Alerts (10 PM)
